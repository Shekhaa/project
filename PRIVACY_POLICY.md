# Privacy Policy — Campus Connect

**Last Updated:** November 27, 2026  
**Effective Date:** November 27, 2026

Welcome to **Campus Connect** ("we", "our", "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy, personal data, and dignity of our campus student community. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use the Campus Connect mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

This policy has been designed in compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** and the **Information Technology Act, 2000** of India.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
* **Account & Profile Data:** Full name, institutional college email address (`@gehu.ac.in`, `@geu.ac.in`, or approved domain), username, password hash, profile avatar, bio, graduation cohort/year, degree program (e.g., B.Tech, MBA), branch/specialization, and admission/roll number.
* **Campus Verification Credentials:** Digital copy or photograph of your student ID card uploaded for institutional verification via OCR.
* **User-Generated Content:** Discussion posts, comments, community chat messages, poll responses, lost & found listings, and uploaded media attachments (photos, videos, audio voice notes, academic PDF notes).
* **Communication & Support:** Messages sent to support, feedback forms, and report submissions.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device & Connection Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, network carrier, and push notification tokens.
* **Usage & Log Data:** Log-in timestamps, features accessed, active sessions, crash telemetry, and error logs.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process student data strictly for legitimate educational, safety, and campus community purposes:
1. **Authentication & Identity Verification:** Verifying active student status to maintain an authentic, closed campus network and awarding official verification badges.
2. **Campus Feed & Networking:** Connecting classmates within the same university, college, branch, or interest community.
3. **Communication Delivery:** Delivering direct peer-to-peer messages, group chats, and real-time push notifications.
4. **Safety, Integrity & Moderation:** Preventing harassment, spam, ragging, unauthorized commercial solicitation, and platform abuse.
5. **App Performance:** Resolving bugs, preventing crashes, and optimizing mobile video/media delivery.

---

## 3. Anonymous Posting & Identity Transparency

Campus Connect provides an **Anonymous Posting feature** (with assigned mascots such as 🦊 Fox or 🦉 Owl) to enable candid campus discussions without public peer judgment:
* **To Other Students:** Your name, avatar, and profile handle are completely hidden. Other students only see the mascot alias.
* **Platform Audit & Safety Exception:** To prevent cyber-bullying, hate speech, threats of violence, or academic malpractice, the system internally associates anonymous posts with your account ID. In cases of severe violations, platform moderators and legal authorities may trace content back to the originating account in accordance with applicable laws.

---

## 4. How We Share & Store Your Data

We **never sell, rent, or trade** student personal data to third-party data brokers or advertisers. Data is shared only with vetted operational infrastructure providers:

* **Cloud Hosting:** Cloud infrastructure (Render, PostgreSQL) located in regional data centers with encrypted storage.
* **Media & Content Delivery:** Cloudinary CDN for serving encrypted media (images, videos, PDF documents).
* **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose data if required by Indian law, court order, regulatory authority, or campus disciplinary committee in response to verified illegal acts or safety emergencies.

---

## 5. Data Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security controls:
* **Encryption in Transit & at Rest:** All API communications use TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption. Passwords are salted and hashed using Argon2 / PBKDF2 with SHA-256.
* **Token Authentication:** Secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT) with restricted lifetimes stored securely in client device storage.
* **Access Control:** Institutional database access is strictly restricted through role-based access controls (RBAC) and connection pooling.
* **Verification ID Protection:** Student ID images submitted for verification are access-restricted and never displayed publicly in student feeds.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

* **Active Accounts:** Data is retained for as long as your account remains active and you are enrolled in the institution.
* **Account Deletion & 14-Day Retention Window:** You can request account deletion at any time via App Settings. Upon initiating deletion:
  * Your profile and posts are immediately hidden from all campus feeds.
  * Your account enters a **14-day retention buffer** during which you can cancel deletion if requested in error.
  * Following 14 days, personal data, ID cards, messages, and profile records are permanently purged from active production databases.

---

## 7. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:
1. **Access:** Request a summary of personal data held about you.
2. **Correction:** Update inaccurate or incomplete academic credentials or profile data.
3. **Withdrawal of Consent / Deletion:** Delete your account and request erasure of your data.
4. **Grievance Redressal:** Submit privacy concerns to our appointed Grievance Officer.
